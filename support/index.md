# Fact Tree support

Fact Tree is an iPhone app of short, true facts. Each one comes from a trusted source and links back
to it.

## Contact us
Email: **facttree.app@gmail.com**

We read every message. Write to us about a problem with the app, a fact you think is wrong, or an
idea for the app.

## Reporting a wrong fact
The quickest way is from the app: Settings › Report a problem / Contact opens an email to us. If you
write from somewhere else, please include:
- the fact's text, or a screenshot of it;
- what you think is wrong, and a source for the correction if you have one.

Every fact carries a link to the source it came from, so you can check it yourself. We correct or
remove facts that turn out to be wrong.

## Reporting a problem with the app
Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version (Settings › General › About), the app version
and what you did just before the problem. An email started from the app's Settings tab already has
the app version in its subject; otherwise it's at the bottom of that tab.

## Privacy
Fact Tree has no account and collects nothing about you. Read the
[privacy policy](https://jessey1207.github.io/facttree-site/privacy-policy/).
